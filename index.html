Sprint 3

Phase 4 – Prototype

Our solution: redesign of the DR app → "DR Sammen"

Concept: DR Sammen

One app and one DR profile that connects DRTV, DR LYD and DR Nyheder – and turns consuming public service into something you do together.

1Value-first onboarding. Browse freely; ask for login when you want to save, continue on TV or personalise. Consent in 3 plain-language toggles.
2"Din dag" feed. News, podcasts and shows mixed for your day – morning brief, commute, evening.
3Sammen rooms. Watch or listen live with friends; react, share, continue on TV.
4Seamless hand-off. Start on the phone, finish on DRTV or in the car via LYD – logged in everywhere.

Supports "Different Together": togetherness, relevance for all ages, and trust through transparent data use.

Phase 5 – Test

Validate, measure, iterate

Test plan

1 · Usability5–8 users aged 15–46 click through the prototype (think-aloud). Task: "Find tonight's show and save it."
2 · Consent A/BA: classic cookie wall. B: value-first toggles. Compare login + consent rate.
3 · DesirabilityWould you open this tomorrow? Rate 1–5. What would make you invite a friend?
4 · IterateFix top 3 friction points, re-test with new users, hand over findings to DR.
